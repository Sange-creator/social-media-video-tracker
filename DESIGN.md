# Design System: Social Media Video Tracker — Studio Monolith

## 1. Visual Atmosphere & Philosophy
An aggressive, ultra-clean studio workstation crafted exclusively for high-output TikTok and Reels creators. Rejecting generic AI rounded bubbles, it employs crisp architectural precision, deep carbon surfaces (`#0B0C10`), high-density editorial data strips, and electric acid-lime (`#E2F952`) & safety-orange (`#FF6B4A`) accents reminiscent of professional Teenage Engineering & Leica hardware interfaces.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Carbon Void** (`#0B0C10`): Main screen canvas with pure depth.
- **Studio Slate** (`#14161E`): Primary structural module background.
- **Titanium Border** (`#252937`): 1px structural hairline divisions.
- **Acid Lime** (`#E2F952`): The singular dominant energy accent for download triggers, completed quotas, and primary sync status.
- **Safety Signal** (`#FF6B4A`): High-priority carry-over alerts, quota deficits, and active recording indicators.
- **Monochrome Chalk** (`#F3F4F6`): High-contrast primary text and editorial numerals.
- **Dark Titanium** (`#9CA3AF`): Monospaced technical metadata (duration, FPS, file size, timestamps).

## 3. Typography Architecture
- **Display Numerals**: Outfit 800 (Extra Bold) with ultra-tight tracking for daily quota indicators (`03 / 05`).
- **Section Headers**: All-caps micro-tracked titles (`TODAY'S PRODUCTION MATRIX`, `ACCOUNT CHANNELS`).
- **Technical Metadata**: Monospaced JetBrains style for timecodes (`13:00 EST`), file dimensions (`1080×1920`), and album destinations.

## 4. Key UI Components
- **Daily Quota Reactor HUD**: Asymmetric split card with a segmented linear step meter, live time zone clock, and instant "Next Drop: 1:00 PM" countdown.
- **Direct Video Filmstrip Tile**: 9:16 vertical poster tiles with integrated top-left status pill (`READY`, `SYNCED`, `POSTED`), duration timecode top-right, and an integrated bottom action bar.
- **Teleprompter Copy Drawer**: Direct 1-tap clipboard sheet interface showing title and hashtag blocks with word/character counters and tactile copy verification.
- **Hardware Floating Dock**: Sleek architectural glass dock with tactile pressed micro-states.
